A collection of small single-file web tools — trackers, calculators, and utilities.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [finance-tracker](finance-tracker/) | Personal finance tracker |
| [video-editor](video-editor/) | **Cutline**, a browser video editor for Reels, Shorts and TikTok |

## Cutline video editor

Open `video-editor/index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Everything runs in the browser; nothing is uploaded.

- **Clips:** trim, split, drag to reorder, speed (0.25×–4×) with ramps, punch-in / push / pull / shake motion, Fill or Fit + blur framing
- **Transitions:** cross-fade, dip to black, flash, zoom, whip
- **Color:** Warm, Golden, Soft, Bright, Moody, Cool, Vintage and B&W looks, plus brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth, fade, vignette and grain
- **Text:** Title, Caption, Bold pop, Script, Step label and Word-by-word styles in seven fonts (including handwritten and Tamil), with pop, fade, slide, float and typewriter animations
- **Captions:** tap-to-time captions from a pasted script, or import `.srt` / `.vtt`
- **Overlays:** emoji stickers, photo overlays with frames, one-click photo collage, numbered step labels
- **Music:** loop, fade, start offset, automatic ducking under speech, beat detection, tap beats (B), snap-to-beat and "cut clips to the beat"
- **Formats:** 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9 with safe-zone guides; export MP4 or WebM at 720p or 1080p
- **Saving:** autosaves in the browser, plus Project › Save / Open for a portable `.zip`

Export records the project in real time, so keep the tab open and in front until it finishes.
